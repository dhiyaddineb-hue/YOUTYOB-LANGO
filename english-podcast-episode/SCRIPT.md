## 4. Explanation · 02:55–05:20 · Sami

For each key phrase: English large → Arabic → one extra example.

1. **What’s your name?** = ما اسمك؟  
   Extra: *My name is Sami.*
2. **Where are you from?** = من أين أنت؟  
   Extra: *I’m from Amman.*
3. **What do you do?** = ماذا تعمل؟ / ما عملك؟  
   Extra: *I’m a student.*
4. **What do you like to do in your free time?** = ماذا تحب أن تفعل في وقت فراغك؟  
   Extra: *I like to cook.*
5. **Nice talking to you.** = سررت بالحديث معك.  
   Extra: *Nice talking to you too.*

## 7. Drill · 08:55–10:40

Sami: Listen. Then it’s your turn.

1. My name is ______.
2. I’m from ______.
3. I’m a ______.
4. I like to ______.
5. Nice talking to you.

Each item: model → 4s gap with “Your turn / دورك الآن” → short confirm.

---

## 8. Quiz · 10:40–11:55

1. How do you ask someone’s name? → What’s your name?
2. How do you ask about their country? → Where are you from?
3. What does “What do you do?” mean? → ماذا تعمل؟
4. How do you end a friendly chat? → Nice talking to you.

Think-time 4s, then the answer card.
